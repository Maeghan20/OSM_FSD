What does a road connection mean?

Roads are drawn as connected line segments. If their endpoints don't meet, a route may fail to continue across the gap. A bridge is different: roads can cross at different heights without joining.

A SMALL BUT IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Connected doesn't always mean touching on the screen.

A bridge may cross over a road without creating a junction. Connecting every crossing can create false turns and inaccurate routing.

Before you choose
  • Does the road visibly continue?
  • Could there be a bridge or tunnel?
  • Are you sure the two segments belong together?

What is a tag?

A tag is a pair: a key describes what kind of information it is, and a value provides that information. For example, highway=residential describes the road type, while name=Oak Street gives its actual name.
